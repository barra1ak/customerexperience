The ag lens

In many industries, a customer can test, return, delay, or replace a product with limited consequence. In agriculture, timing, weather, biology, inventory, and local support can turn a normal buying decision into a high-stakes operational decision.

🌦️
External uncertaintyWeather, pests, disease pressure, markets, and supply chains can change the “right” decision quickly.
🗓️
Compressed decision windowsPlanting, application, and harvest windows make speed and dependability part of the value proposition.
📍
Local fit mattersThe same product can feel different depending on soil type, weather patterns, equipment, labor, crop, and management style.
💬
Relationships carry memoryCustomers remember who showed up during stressful moments, not just who offered the lowest price.
